How Oxygen Salon carries a service choice into booking

Visitors can begin with a service category or bring a specific service straight into the appointment form.

On the services page, someone can browse categories such as Hair, Nails, or Skin before choosing a specific treatment. Each treatment has a Book this service link. That link passes the category and service into the form, so the visitor starts at date and time with both choices already filled in.

Two ways into the same form

Visitors who open booking directly start by choosing a category and then a service. Both paths lead to contact details and a review step, where visitors can check what they entered before requesting an appointment.

The current site demonstrates the flow through the review step. Online appointment requests are awaiting a connection.
